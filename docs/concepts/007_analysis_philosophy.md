# 007 — Analysis Philosophy

## Purpose

Define what kinds of analysis the application may perform and how that analysis should be framed.

The application analyzes personal, relational, therapeutic, and professional conversations. It may use psychological and behavioral lenses, but it must remain evidence-based, reflective, and non-diagnostic.

---

# Core Analysis Principle

The application does not judge people.

The application reflects conversation evidence.

It distinguishes:

```text
what was said
what may be happening
what is insufficiently supported
what a user might reflect on
```

---

# Permitted Analytical Lenses

The application may include lenses inspired by:

- communication analysis
- behavioral analysis
- cognitive reflection
- CBT-style thought pattern review
- DBT-style emotion regulation and interpersonal effectiveness reflection
- psychoanalytic or psychodynamic hypothesis generation
- attachment-informed interaction analysis
- trauma-informed communication review
- mediation and conflict mapping
- NVC observations, feelings, needs, and requests
- systems thinking
- narrative identity and meaning-making
- bias and reliability audit
- safety-aware review

---

# Important Boundary

These lenses are used for reflection.

They are not used to diagnose, treat, or clinically evaluate a person.

---

# Psychological Hypothesis Handling

The product may help evaluate whether transcript evidence is consistent with a hypothesis.

Example hypotheses:

- avoidant withdrawal
- schizoid-like distancing
- borderline-like fear of abandonment
- narcissistic injury hypothesis
- trauma-triggered reactivity
- depressive withdrawal
- shame defense
- cognitive distortion
- black-and-white framing
- mind reading
- projection hypothesis
- anxious pursuit
- emotional invalidation

For each hypothesis, the system should classify evidence as:

```text
supports
partially supports
contradicts
insufficient evidence
alternative explanation likely
```

It should not say:

```text
diagnosed
confirmed
proved
is a narcissist
has BPD
has SPD
is abusive
is manipulative
```

---

# Therapeutic Reflection

The application may generate reflection points such as:

- CBT-style thought review
- DBT-style regulation/interpersonal effectiveness reflection
- communication repair opportunities
- needs clarification
- boundary reflection
- assumption checking
- emotional validation opportunities
- behavior-impact reflection

It should avoid:

- treatment plans
- clinical advice
- medication discussion
- diagnosis
- crisis counseling as a substitute for emergency help
- directives that pressure reconciliation

---

# Bias, Prejudice, and Behavior

The application may identify possible evidence of:

- bias
- prejudice
- stereotyping
- unfair attribution
- mind reading
- hostile interpretation
- dismissive language
- contempt-like phrasing
- avoidance
- escalation
- defensiveness
- stonewalling-like withdrawal
- invalidation

But it must frame these as evidence-limited observations.

Example:

```text
This statement may reflect a hostile attribution because it assumes intent without direct evidence.
```

Not:

```text
The speaker is prejudiced.
```

---

# Safety-Aware Analysis

When safety-relevant content appears, the system should shift posture.

Examples:

- threats
- intimidation
- stalking
- monitoring
- coercion
- severe control
- self-harm statements
- fear of harm
- forced isolation
- repeated humiliation

In those cases, the system should:

- avoid mutualizing serious concerns
- avoid pressuring repair/reconciliation
- flag evidence carefully
- encourage appropriate professional or emergency support when warranted
- maintain non-adjudicative but safety-aware framing

---

# Longitudinal Analysis

Longitudinal analysis should ask:

- Does this pattern recur?
- Does the intensity change?
- Does repair improve?
- Do reflection points repeat?
- Do hypotheses gain or lose support?
- Are there new contradictions?
- Is the user’s own behavior changing?
- Are safety indicators increasing or decreasing?

Longitudinal claims require multiple transcript/version references.

---

# Core Analysis Invariants

- Every claim links to evidence or is marked as uncertainty.
- Interpretations are separated from observations.
- Psychological hypotheses remain hypotheses.
- User-provided diagnoses are treated as context, not proof.
- Findings include confidence.
- Safety framing overrides ordinary coaching framing.
- Longitudinal conclusions require longitudinal evidence.
- The user remains the decision-maker.
